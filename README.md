# FireLab Support

FireLab is an early retirement calculator for Australians. It works out when you could retire, and how to split your investing between super and non-super investments to get there.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many free calculations do I get?**
Three. A calculation only uses one up if it produces a retirement plan. If your debts couldn't be cleared, it doesn't count, and re-running the exact same details doesn't count twice. After that, a one-time purchase unlocks unlimited calculations. There's no subscription.

**I bought the unlock but the app is asking me to pay again.**
Open the unlock screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your device. FireLab has no accounts and doesn't send your details anywhere. It keeps your last 3 calculations so you can revisit them. Deleting the app deletes everything. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).

**Why does my result say my debts weren't cleared?**
Either your minimum debt repayments cost more than you have left after your living and personal expenses, or your debts couldn't be paid off before age 67. Try lowering your expenses or personal spending, or check your loan details.

**What does "brokerage" mean in FireLab?**
Any investment that isn't super, such as shares, ETFs, bonds or an investment property. It doesn't have to be a brokerage account.

**Is FireLab financial advice?**
No. FireLab is a simplified estimator based on the assumptions you enter. Real outcomes depend on future tax law, market returns and your circumstances. Speak to a licensed financial adviser before acting on anything in the app.
