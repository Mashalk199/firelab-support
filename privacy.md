# FireLab Privacy Policy

_Effective 29 September 2026_

FireLab doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal information. Everything you enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores, and where

To work, FireLab keeps the following **on your device only**:

- The details you enter, such as your date of birth, income, expenses, loans, super and investments.
- Your last 3 calculations, so you can revisit them.
- A count of how many free calculations you've used.

None of this is sent to the developer or to anyone else. FireLab has no accounts, no servers and no online storage of its own.

If you have device backups turned on (such as iCloud Backup), your phone may include FireLab's data in those backups. Apple handles those backups, not FireLab.

## What the app doesn't do

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising
- No third-party tracking or data-collection tools
- No sharing or selling of data

## Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. FireLab never sees your payment details. The app only asks Apple whether the unlock has been purchased on your Apple ID.

## Sharing your results

If you choose to share or export your results as a PDF, it goes only where you send it using your phone's share options. FireLab doesn't keep a copy anywhere else.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app, and all of FireLab's data is removed from your device.

## Children

FireLab isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
